    How the standings work
    • Won is games won out of games played, and Win % is the share you won. Win % lists players with at least three games first.
    • Outlasted counts the opponents who went out before you. Win and you’ve outlasted the whole table, so a win at a table of five counts 4. Come 2nd at a table of four and you’ve outlasted 2. Players knocked out together don’t count each other, and games logged without knockouts only count the winner’s.
    • vs average compares your wins with an average player’s, based on player count. An average player wins 1 game in 4 at a table of four and 1 in 3 at a table of three. Above zero means you win more than average, so it’s fair on people who have played fewer games.

        What’s new

        • A game timer at the top of Log a game: start it when the game begins, stop it at the end and the length fills itself in
        • Same table again: after saving a game, or with Again on any game, the next one starts with the same players and decks
        • The colours change: each day the page takes on a different deck that’s doing well, and on game night it’s the latest winner’s. Tap the line under the pod’s name for another.
        • Commander picks that know how you play: how you win and knock people out, how you go out, the cards that won your games, how each deck does and what wins at your table, matched to thousands of real decks. Open “What these picks are based on” to see it. Save one for more like it, or Not for me for fewer.
        • Discover is back (in the bar along the bottom on phones): roll a random commander, take the commander quiz (now on real deck data, with a question on how serious your table is), and tap Save to keep a shortlist
